Our origin story

NO FOCUS GAMING began as a weekend LAN party and grew into a full gaming store and arena for the whole community.

Origin story

NO FOCUS GAMING opened in 2019 with eight borrowed monitors and a big idea: every gamer deserves a fast, friendly place to play, shop and compete.

Today we run two arenas, a retail floor stocked with consoles and PC gear, and a community of more than five thousand registered players.

Mission

Make gaming social, fair and welcoming by putting great gear and great people in one room.

Vision

To be the home base for gaming communities across South Africa, online and in person.

Target audience

Players aged 13 to 35, families shopping for gifts, and clubs or schools that want a safe place to compete.
